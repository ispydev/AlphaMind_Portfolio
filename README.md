## 📌 Project Overview

Managing marketing across a dynamic, multi-venue lifestyle and hospitality portfolio requires balancing distinct brand identities with tight operational deadlines. Instead of submitting a static cover letter, I built this **Multi-Venue Campaign & Launch Command Center** to demonstrate how structured project management, multi-vendor coordination, and performance tracking translate into seamless hospitality marketing execution.

---

## ✨ Key Interactive Modules

1. **Multi-Brand Campaign Portfolio Matrix**
   - Switch between flagship AlphaMind concepts—**CLAP Dubai**, **KIRA**, **Iris Dubai**, and **Son of a Fish**—to explore tailored campaign themes, target personas, channel mixes, and interactive launch readiness checklists.
2. **6-Stage Hospitality Launch & Event Critical Path**
   - Interactive milestone tracker simulating end-to-end event execution (from *Concept & Positioning* through *Menu Visual Shoots*, *PR & VIP Seeding*, *Paid Social Rollout*, *Venue Alignment*, and *Live Recap*), complete with ownership mapping and live exception/risk logging.
3. **Scroll-Stopping Content & Venue Tone Lab**
   - A 9:16 vertical social preview demonstrating how hook writing, visual direction, and brand tone adapt across venues alongside a pre-publish Brand & Compliance QA checklist.
4. **Guest Engagement & Footfall Impact Simulator**
   - Real-time campaign funnel calculator modeling how social reach, engagement rate, reservation link CTR, and average spend per cover (AED) translate into incremental bookings and projected revenue impact.
